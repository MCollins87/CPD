## Activity Overview

The second of three required training to become nominated Clinical Safety Officer. The others being "[Essentials of Digital Clinical Safety](./2024-09-04_EDCS.md)" and "[Clinical Safety Officer](./2025-03-25_CSO.md)".

This course outlines what processes need to be followed, what activities need to be undertaken and what documentation needs to be produced to help ensure digital clinical safety.

### Objectives
- How to apply the safety standards in practice
- The documents, processes and activities required to comply with the standards
- Roles and responsibilities within the clinical risk management process.


## Benefits to me

To work towards being a qualified and recognised Clinical Safety Officer. Understanding safer digital clinical practice and ability to implement safety by design products.

## Benefits to Service 

Compliance with DCB0160.
Digital service improvements are implemented with patient safety as a key consideration.
