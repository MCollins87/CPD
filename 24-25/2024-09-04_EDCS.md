## Activity Overview

The first of three required training to become nominated Clinical Safety Officer. The others being [Digital Clinical Safety. Intermediate](./2024-09-05_DCS_I.md) and [Clinical Safety Officer](./2025-03-25_CSO.md). 
This e-learning is suitable for clinical and non-clinical staff. All staff working within  and for Health and Social Care organisations will benefit from completing this training.

### Objectives
- Describe what digital clinical safety is
- Understand the legal context of the digital clinical safety standards
- Understand what is required for an effective Digital clinical safety process
- Recognise the importance of digital clinical safety


## Benefits to me

Understand the requirements for digital clinical safety and why it is important.


## Benefits to Service 

Working towards having a dedicated clinical safety officer within the department to ensure digital service improvements are implemented safely.
