# Management Essentials

| Date | 25/01/2024 - 20/06/2024 |
| --- | --- |

| Provider | Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust |

## Activity Overview

This multi-module course, run by PHU provides managers and leaders with the essential skills to manage teams and align departments with the Trust strategy and goals. Modules include:
- PHU Strategic Apporach
- Governance, Risk, Analytics and Patient safety and experience
- Recruitment
- Appraisals
- Managing People
- Managing Mental Health of your team
- Working Flexibly
- Combined Coaching and Mentoring Skills
- Finance for Budget Managers
- Contracts and Activity
- Report writing
- Chairing a meeting
- Procurement
- Improvement


## Benefits to me

Better knowledge of Trust strategic direction. Also knowlege and skills gained to perform as a key member of the Radiotherapy Senior Leadership Team.


## Benefits to Service 

The knowlege gained through these modules allowed the Radiotherapy 10 year strategy to be alligned with the Trust vision, and helped with approval at Trust Board.
