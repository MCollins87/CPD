## Activity Overview

The third of three required training to become nominated Clinical Safety Officer. The others being "[Essentials of Digital Clinical Safety](./2024-09-04_EDCS.md)" and "[Digital Clinical Safety. Intermediate](./2024-09-05_DCS_I.md).

This on-line training over MS Teams provides the practical knowledge and understanding to undertake the role of a Digital Clinical Safety Officer.

Practical experience of completing Clinical Risk Management activities.
