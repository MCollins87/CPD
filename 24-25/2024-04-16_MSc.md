## WHAT - Activity Overview

Masters in Enterprise IT Management. This Course has covered the following modules:
- April 2024, "Launching into Enterprise IT Management", 20 credits, 180 Hrs
- July 2024, "Security and Risk Management", 20 credits, 180 Hrs
- October 2024, "Information Technology Service Management", 10 credits, 90 Hrs
- January 2025, "Enterprise IT Project Management", 10 credits, 90 Hrs

Full details can be found: [MSc Enterprise IT Management](https://mcollins87.github.io/MSC_EnterpriseIT/)

## SO WHAT - Benefits to me

This MSc is providing greater understanding and practivcal experience of service management, particuarly from an IT perspective. 

The modules covered so far have provided insight into Information Technology service life-cycles, risk management and project management, in particular how IT projects differ from other projects.


## WHAT NOW - Benefits to Service 

The learning from this course has been implemented in my daily working life. For example, the knowledge goained regarding IT serice life-cycles has fed directly into the development of the Radiotherapy 10 year strategy. 

The Project Management module has also provided adidtional tools I have implemented in existing projects.
